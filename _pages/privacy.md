This is a static research website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not provide user accounts or intentionally collect information submitted by visitors.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and improve its content. Analytics is loaded only according to the site's consent settings. Google may process technical information such as browser type, approximate location, referring page, and pages visited. You can decline analytics through the consent controls or use browser-based tracking protection.

External links are governed by the privacy policies of their respective websites. Questions about this site can be sent to the email address listed on the [home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).
