This is a static research website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not provide user accounts or intentionally collect information submitted by visitors.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and improve its content. Analytics loads when you visit a page and may set analytics cookies. Google may process technical information such as browser type, approximate location, referring page, and pages visited. You can prevent this collection by using browser-based tracking protection or an analytics blocker.

External links are governed by the privacy policies of their respective websites. Questions about this site can be sent to the email address listed on the [home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).
